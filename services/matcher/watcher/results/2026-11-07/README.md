# First live watch: 7 November 2026, 03:36–04:59 UTC

`summary.md` was generated by `watcher/watch.py` from `launches.jsonl.gz`, which has one line per launch with its
signature and slot, so every launch can be checked on-chain. `events.log` holds the connection events.

**Coverage:**
- Free endpoints only: the Solana Foundation websocket and PublicNode HTTP.
- 1.41 hours, with two gaps: about 1 minute at 04:10, when the container restarted and the watcher resumed from the
  log, and 1 second at 04:16, a websocket drop.
- The run ended at 04:59 when the container restarted again.
- All pump.fun launches decoded from logs. No create was seen without being decoded (see the decoding lines in
  `summary.md`).

**Findings:**
- **Copy volume.** 1,870 launches. 1,034 (55.3%) would have been a door of a room opened earlier in the same
  window. 615 of those came from a different wallet than the room's door 0; the other 419 were the same wallet
  relaunching (for example "Pepe Wif Sword", 93 launches from 2 wallets).
- **Copied metadata.** 250 of the 615 copies by other wallets reuse door 0's exact metadata URI: same image and
  metadata, copied outright.
- **Copy platforms.** LaunchLab platforms mostly copied pump.fun narratives:
  - pump2: 28 of 32 launches would be doors.
  - Float: 9 of 12.
  - StonkFun (self-declared configs): 9 of 16. All 9 were other wallets' copies of narratives started on pump.fun
    or Float ("Scamoors", "scamlon", "Max"), except one ("PEMP") of a StonkFun room.
  - 53 doors cross platforms.

**Precision spot check (by me, not human-reviewed):** 40 randomly drawn copies by other wallets (seed 7).
- All 40 have the same name after folding: 532 of the 615 match symbol too (T3), 83 the name alone (T2n).
- About 9 of the 40 are generic words ("beer", "dog", "Max", "otc", "wom", "Croc", "LUCKY", "meme"/"memecoin",
  "scam"/"SCAM COIN"). Two independent people could pick those; on our launchpad they would share a room by design.
- No pair in the sample was a different name wrongly merged.

**Limits:**
- Rooms exist only from the start of the watch, so the door share rose from 52.3% in the first half to 58.1% in the
  second.
- Rooms are all treated as live.
- 83 minutes is one sample of one morning (UTC).
- Escapes are not counted. "The Scamooors" opened its own room next to "Scamoors"; that is the typo class the
  evasion benchmark measures.
